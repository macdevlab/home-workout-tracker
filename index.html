Exercise modifications

Push-ups

Wall push-up → sturdy counter → sofa/bench → knee push-up → floor push-up. Use a stable surface and keep your body in a straight line.

Squats and lunges

Use a chair or hold a sturdy support. Squat to a comfortable depth; replace reverse lunges with supported split squats or sit-to-stands if needed.

Back and arms

Use a backpack with a few books or water bottles for rows, curls and hip hinges. Start light and keep the load close. Avoid unstable furniture or improvised door anchors.

Plank and mountain climbers

Do the plank from your knees or with hands on a counter. For mountain climbers, stand and march briskly if floor work is uncomfortable.

Training principles

  • Build the habit before increasing difficulty.
  • Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain or unusual breathlessness.
  • Keep at least one easy recovery day and one full rest day each week.
  • Fat loss comes from overall energy balance; abdominal exercises cannot target belly fat specifically.
  • Start below the listed volume if you are new to exercise, and progress at a pace that feels manageable.
